# FUTURE_DS_01
Business Sales Performance Analytics – Future Interns Task 1
## Project Overview
This project analyzes business sales data using Power BI to understand sales performance, product performance, category performance, regional performance, and sales trends.

## Tools Used
- Power BI
- Superstore Sales Dataset

## Key Insights
- Technology is the highest-selling category.
- West is the highest-performing region by sales.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the top-selling product among the Top 10 products.
- Total sales are approximately 2.30M.
- 2015 recorded the highest sales during the period shown in the dashboard.
- The dashboard also provides a profit performance view.

## Recommendations
- Focus more on the Technology category to improve overall sales.
- Strengthen sales and marketing activities in the West region.
- Promote top-selling products to increase sales further.
- Monitor sales trends regularly to support better business decisions.
- Use sales and profit insights for better product and regional planning.

## Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to analyze sales by category, region, product, and time.
